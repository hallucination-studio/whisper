# Require evidence before research changes production semantics

Whisper treats papers, external projects, available runtimes, prototypes, and
lower self-supervised loss as evidence inputs rather than behavior authorities.
A future research path receives a versioned contract and implementation issue
graph only after independent holdout, provenance, resource, failure, fallback,
and replay evidence supports an explicit maintainer decision; changing
production world semantics additionally requires semantic ground truth and a
selected fusion or replacement rule. This slows experimental adoption but
prevents a convenient model or metric from silently redefining replayable world
state. The promotion sequence lives in the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md#promotion-rule),
and source identity lives in the [references index](../references/README.md).
